The problem

Your ERP has the data. Your team still digs for the answer.

Owners and staff manually read ERP exports, predefined reports, and operational tables every day. The important signals are buried inside those tables: expiry risks, stock movement, pricing pressure, seasonality, and delayed deliveries.

  • 1Expiry risk hides inside batches. Stock becomes urgent before the right person sees the problem.
  • 2Slow-moving products stay invisible. Velocity drops only appear after someone reads the report.
  • 3Customer updates need chasing. Teams switch between orders, warehouse status, and delivery information.

“By the time someone has read the answer out of the report, the window to act has often passed.”
